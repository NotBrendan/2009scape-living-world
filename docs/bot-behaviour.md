# Bot behaviour: the technical reference

A reference for every bot behaviour in the add-ons, with the server flag that controls each one and its default. Written from the code as it ran on the owner's live server in October and November 2026, so a few sections still read like a log; the flags and numbers are current.

Source: `deployment/addons-src/content/custom/*.java`. Server flags are `-D` properties in the service's start script; all defaults are listed.

---

## 0. Rules every bot follows
1. **Nothing from nothing.** Coins only arrive by selling something (FIFO ledger), from a one-off nest egg at first spawn, from the clerk fund (stale stock, capped), or from another bot/player paying. No bot is paid at the bank booth (fixed 11:53 UTC today; before that bots were paid twice).
2. **Not loot piñatas.** Bots avoid deaths they can see coming (levels, poison, magic, shared death memory). When they die they lose what a player loses.
3. **If you can watch it, it should look like a person.** Names stick, wallets are real coins in the bank, shifts, moods, chat, crews, shops on foot.

4. **Nobody stays stuck (live 10-21 01:58, `Watchdog.java`, `-Dwatchdog.min`, default 20).** Every 30 s the watchdog checks the travelling bots (smart adventurers, traders, the herb gatherer). One that has stayed within 8 tiles for 20 min with no XP gained, isn't at a bank, the Exchange or a hangout, and is trying to get somewhere (walking to a spot, bank, shop or the Exchange, on a route, or recently stuck) is teleported free to the nearest bank (teleport animation, a line like "Finally! Out of there."), its task ends so it picks a new one, and the spot goes in `data/eco/traps2.json` (two rescues make it a trap; in a trap it waits only 5 min). A bot merely idle at a work spot for 20 min gives up the job and walks off instead (from 03:06). Traps and the Ardougne zoo enclosures are never hunt spots, and long-trip and stuck teleports aimed at one land at the nearest bank instead. The server's own skilling bots, artisans and town folk are left alone (they stand still on purpose).

Populations (restart 18:31 UTC): 90 smart adventurers + 12 high tier (10 target; 2 extra minted before the restore fix, all kept), 9 artisans, 4 travelling traders, 8 town locals, 1 fish sweeper, 1 herb gatherer, 35 map surveyors, 5 old "dumb" adventurers (`server.conf max_adv_bots = 5`).

## 1. Smart adventurers (`SmartAdventurers.java`)
**Who:** 90 bots (`-Dsmartbots.count`, 90). 3 in 4 spawn fresh (LOW tier gear, gathering skills 10–35, 600 gp), 1 in 4 veteran (MED tier, gathering 55–90, a third of them Mining 85–94, 20–60k gp). Every bot has a **home town** (one of 8), a **favourite trade** (veteran miners → Mining; 1 in 5 → fighting; else its best gathering skill), a **catchphrase** and a **grudge**, a **goal item**, and a saved **profile** (`data/smartbots.json`, saved every 10 min, including bots not yet spawned this boot and bots on a break: name, levels, **exact XP per skill**, gear ids, coins, home, trade, goal, story, high-tier flag).

**Levelling:** bots are marked as having finished Tutorial Island (the server caps XP at 175 for anyone who hasn't; until 16:08 UTC 2026-10-20 this silently stopped every bot from levelling). They gain XP the same way players do, from the game's own skill code on every real action (each ore mined, fish caught, kill, pickpocket, bone buried, sawmill build). Levels and XP are restored exactly after a restart or a break.

**Identity**
- Comes back after a restart under the **same name** (`namedBot`: writes `data/botdata/smart-<base>`, sets the server's name counter). Names with 5+ digit tails are refused (a fresh name is taken, levels kept); the counter never goes above 999 because of a restore.
- Returning bot with no saved gear and under 600 gp gets the newcomer's start money (nobody comes back naked).

**Gear** (`payForGear`, `wear`, armour trips): every trip starts with a look at its kit: missing helm, body, legs, shield or weapon are bought on foot at the armour shop that sells most of them, as a matched set at the best tier its Attack/Defence allow (bronze, iron, steel, mithril, adamant, rune where a shop sells it) and its purse can stand (≤ 60% of its coins); it never replaces what it wears. At spawn: the assembler's gear is "bought": pieces under 200 gp count as shop purchases; anything dearer must be taken from Exchange stock via the ledger (paying the supplier) or is removed. Empty weapon/body/legs/helm slots are filled from "the shop" (coins removed): **bronze** for a bot under 5k gp (a newcomer keeps most of its 600 gp), iron for one with savings. Dragon hunts need an anti-dragon/dragonfire shield worn (1540/11283/11284).

**Jobs** (`Brain.chooseJob`, every 4 s when idle): candidate jobs = gathering tasks with known spots + every monster family from `HuntMap`. Score = bounty weight of its products (§5) ÷ (1 + distance/150; a dungeon spot is measured from the ground above it) × random 0.8–1.2, halved for the last job, divided by (1 + bots already there). Multipliers: ×3 for the classic 2009 monsters (blue/green/red/black dragons, baby blue dragons, black/lesser/greater demons, fire/moss/hill/ice giants, hellhounds, ice warriors); ×1.6 favourite skill; ×(1 + 0.25 × recipes an artisan is short of, max 4); crews ×1.5 for fights; high tier ×3 for hunts of level 80+, ×1.5 for 40+, ×0.2 for anything under level 40. Skips: level too low (hunts need combat ≥ npcLevel × 1.3 + 5 when npcLevel < 80; for npcLevel ≥ 80 that rule is REPLACED by combat ≥ 90 plus Prayer ≥ 43 and a dragonfire shield, since 1.3 × 111 + 5 would exceed the combat cap); spot at capacity (adamant 2, mithril/gold/silver 2, runite 1, coal/iron 6, hunts = spawns/3 capped 1–4, else 4); job label blacklisted 24 h after the bot died there; **poisonous** monsters (snake, tribesman, scorpion, poison/giant/jungle/deadly red spider, cave slime, kalphite, wall beast, bloodveld) need combat 70; **shared death memory**: 2 deaths at a spot in 24 h close it to all bots for a day, each recent death adds +10 to the level wanted; **magic users** (mage, wizard, sorcerer, necromancer, shaman, druid) need combat ≥ 2 × npcLevel + 10. **Broke** (< 400 gp): scores jobs by expected coins for one trip (gathering: best product × 20; hunts: drop table's expected value per kill × 15, so a rare valuable drop no longer wins; pickpocketing: coins on the spot, counted double since nothing waits in a queue: men 3, farmers 9, Al Kharid warriors 18 per steal × 40) and says "Skint. Need coin for a new pick. X it is." (once an hour). Pickpocket spots: Lumbridge men, Varrock square, Draynor villagers, Draynor market farmers, Al Kharid warriors.

**Before a trip** (`start`): if the job needs a tool it lacks or (fights/thieving/hunts) it has < 8 food → **shop trip** (`planShopTrip`): nearest of 161 real shops within 600 tiles stocking the tool (required) and/or a food (lobster, trout, salmon, bread, shrimps, cake, cooked meat), says "Need a pickaxe first. Off to Bob's.", walks in 25-tile hops (hop points snapped onto walkable ground; sideways 18-tile detour after two hops with no progress), teleports (paid) if > 320 tiles and ≥ 5k gp (after 4 stalled hops or half the deadline it teleports anyway if it can afford it), faces the keeper, pays guide price × amount, items into bank/inventory; 8-min deadline then falls back to stock/nominal purchase. No shop in range → tools from Exchange stock if > 5 in stock (paying supplier) else a bronze/iron one paid to "the shop"; food 10 trout/salmon/lobster from stock or shop.
- Trip size: between 14 and 56 units (`-Dsmartbots.tripMax`), up to what the market is short of. Fish are cooked before selling, except: a bot whose trade is fishing banks the catch raw 60% of the time, and a bot whose Cooking is below the fish's level always banks it raw (so cook artisans get raw fish to split). Haul is walked to a bank within 220 tiles and banked, then: **bank arrival** → `Deals.arrive` (see §9), else straight to the Exchange via the ledger.
- Teleport = a law rune + 3 airs at guide (min 100 gp), paid, only for legs over 320 tiles (`-Dtele.min`); no coins → walks. Bots under 5k gp never teleport (`walker`). Dungeon spots with a town entrance are walked into: Taverley (teleport to Falador, walk west past the bank to the ladder at 2884,3396), Edgeville dungeon (trapdoor by the bank), Karamja volcano (Brimhaven side); the bot climbs down and walks on. Other dungeons still teleport straight to the spot.
- **Agility** (`agilityTick`): bots under Agility 35 now and then (1 in 8 job choices, at most every 4 h, not when broke or in a crew) go to the Gnome Stronghold course and run 10 real laps (log, net, branch, rope, branch, net, pipe) for up to 45 min, earning XP the normal way. Veterans and the high tier are given a level that matches their combat when they next spawn (high 60–75, combat 90+ 48–62, 60+ 30–47, veteran/40+ 15–30), only while still at 1. When the route map exists, a bot turned away from a shortcut gets sent to train (`agilityNudge`).
- Fight and hunt trips by a bot under 43 Prayer bury the bones they collect (veterans and fighters always, fresh bots one trip in two), so Prayer climbs slowly like a player's; at 43+ bones are sold. Prayer only gates the level 60+ hunts, which only the high tier (Prayer 52–70 from spawn) takes.
- Eats at 60% health (players 50%). Prayer: hunts of npcLevel ≥ 60 with Prayer ≥ 43 keep Protect from Melee on in combat, off between fights; under 8 points a prayer potion (2434) is bought from stock or the shop. Since batch 11 (10-23 12:39) the eat check and the "one more hit could kill you" check run on every pulse with a 3-tick bite cooldown (a player eats every 3 ticks; before, a bot was blind for 6 to 9 ticks after each attack), the thresholds use the sum of the max hits of everyone attacking it, and fight food is chosen by the target (swordfish for level 172, trout for low levels). With no food anywhere the trip ends ("Out of food."; at most 2 emergency teleports per trip, to the nearest bank it may enter at any distance, paid once); a desert trip needs a waterskin and ends "Out of water." when they run out.

**Death** (`checkDeath`): bots die through the server's own player death code: they keep their 3 most valuable items (in the pack on respawn) and leave a gravestone with the rest where they fell. Bot found at the Lumbridge respawn while its trip was > 40 tiles away = died: logs `DEATH`, blacklists the job 24 h, adds to shared death memory, finishes the trip, says a grumble, news line, crewmates say they'll bless the grave, mood GRUMPY 30 min. Then a **grave run**: unless that spot has killed bots twice today, it walks (or pays to teleport, if over 60 tiles) back to its gravestone within 4 minutes, takes everything back ("Got it all back. Phew."), lets the empty stone crumble, wears its kept and recovered gear, and buys only what is still missing (bronze/iron from the shop). Too late or no grave: wears the kept 3 and buys the gaps. **Since batch 11 (10-23 12:39) this is true for NPC kills too** (before, a bot killed by an NPC respawned with every item and full health, because the engine skips the death steps for artificial players killed by NPCs): player-rules deaths are ON by default (the owner, 11:25 UTC); switch off with `-Dbots.deathLoss=false` or the file `data/bots.deathloss.off` (re-read every minute). Each bot has a unique account uid for its grave, a death is read from the engine's death state (so an NPC kill near Lumbridge counts), the spot memory survives restarts (`data/eco/death-memory.json`), the DEATH line carries tier, combat level, killer and food, and a dead trip's runner stops at once (a haul in the pack goes to bot stock). A grave run starts only when the grave exists and ends at the stone's own timer (2 minutes); too-far, timed-out and crumbled runs are logged. Batch 17 (live 2026-10-25): each bot has its own uid (0x40000000 + AIPlayer uid), so stones are per bot; kept worn items are worn again after the respawn (`wearKept`); items in a crumbled stone are destroyed (`Grave.collapse` -> `GroundItemManager.destroy`).

**Day shape**
- **Shift** 2–5 h (FAST 4–7 min; `-Dsmartbots.shiftSec`). At the first trip end after it, if < 40% of bots are away: says goodnight, logs out for 1–4 h (`-Dsmartbots.awaySec`), profile kept in memory and in the save file. Its ledger lots stay live; sales while away accrue (`Ledger.Wallet.OFFLINE`, persisted) and are collected on return ("is back, collected N gp").
- **Breaks:** every 2–4 trips, 1–3 min at the Grand Exchange: shuffles about, idle lines (catchphrase 1 in 4, goal talk, mood lines), **direct deals** (§9), **spectacle** (§10).
- **Goal** (`GOALS`; only goals the bot can actually get: in Exchange stock, or sold by a real specialty shop it qualifies for; if the Exchange has none it walks to the shop, e.g. Seddus for rune platelegs/plateskirt, Rasolo for a dragon dagger; Oziach, Skulgrimen and Daga are high tier only because of their quests): rune scimitar/helm/body/legs/kite/boots (Att or Def 40), glory, obsidian cape, berserker helm (Def 45), dragon long/scim/sq/boots (60), whip (70). Chosen when its price is above the bot's coins and under 40× them. Bought from stock when coins ≥ price + 1,000 (paying the supplier), worn, celebrated, new goal picked.
- **Moods:** CHATTY 10 min after a haul ≥ 20 (15 after buying a goal), GRUMPY 30 min after a death, TIRED in the last quarter of a shift. Change set-out and idle lines.
- **Plank run** (builders = veteran or high tier with ≥ 15k gp for oak, ≥ 40k for teak and mahogany; 1 in 5 trip ends, at most every 90 min): 27 logs from the bank or the Exchange (needs 40 on the Exchange); short of oak, the builder asks out loud and a woodcutter within 150 tiles takes it as an errand and delivers for 95% of guide; → Sawmill Operator (fee 250/500/1,500 per log, coins destroyed) → Rimmington house portal → builds 1–2 min: planks removed for good (+Construction XP); at most 20% / 10 planks sold as notes. Gives up after 12 min stuck.

**Rangers and mages** (from 20:54): about 35% of fighters (trade "fighting", including the high tier) are rangers (18%) or mages (17%), chosen from the name so it never changes. Their Ranged or Magic is brought up to their Attack once if they never trained it. **Rangers** wear coif/leather or green d'hide (Ranged and Defence 40) and the best shortbow their level allows (normal, oak, willow, maple) with the best arrows that bow fires (bronze to adamant), 200 at a time. **Mages** wear a staff of fire, wizard hat and blue skirt (robe top only if someone has made one) and autocast fire bolt (Magic 35+), fire strike (13+) or wind strike, carrying runes for 150 casts; out of runes mid-trip they say so and fight with the staff. Kit, arrows and runes come from Exchange stock first (paying the fletcher, crafter or runecrafter through the ledger), then from real shops on foot (Lowe's, Brian's, Dargaud's, the Ranging Guild, Zaff's staff shop, Aubury, Betty, Ali); if a shop can't be reached they're bought "elsewhere" (coins leave the game). Arrows, runes and food in the pack and bank are kept over a restart and a break (persistence, 22:30, bugs-fixed #46). Players can sell them arrows and runes too. Trial: a ranger gained Ranged XP shooting cows; a mage cast 13 fire bolts at cows (+452 Magic XP, 13 chaos and 39 air used).

**From 21:26 (the owner's notes):** **Town hangouts:** half of all breaks are at the nearest of 12 hangouts (Lumbridge courtyard, Varrock fountain, Falador park, the Rising Sun Inn, Draynor market, Port Sarim docks, Catherby beach, Edgeville well, Al Kharid palace gardens, Seers' bank, the Barbarian Village fire, Ardougne market) instead of the Exchange, walked to; while there bots use emotes by mood (yawn and think when tired, laugh/cheer/wave/clap/dance when chatty, angry when grumpy). **Shooting stars:** when a star is down, up to 5 miners (trade mining, level high enough, with a pickaxe) announce it, go there and mine it for about 5 minutes, chatting; stardust goes to their bank. **Crier rallies:** when the Varrock crier calls the board, up to 3 idle adventurers within 15 tiles answer ("Bonus on coal? I'm on it.") and go for an item on it they can fetch. **Trust:** each player builds trust with each bot (+1 for buying its callout, selling it something it needed, or a chat, once a day; saved in `data/botchat-trust.json`); at 3+ the bot greets you by name in a bank ("Alright the owner, back from the mines?") and its callouts give you mates' rates (88% of guide) with 10 s more before another bot can claim. Mages at Magic 59+ cast fire blast with death runes.

**Human habits** (from 20:54): at the bank a bot stands at the booth 5–10 s before depositing; when its rock or tree runs out it goes AFK for 1–4 s (sometimes a step or a turn) before the next one; (the roadside breathers on long walks were removed at 21:26 at the owner's request: emotes happen at the hangouts instead); in bot banter a third bot nearby sometimes joins in.

**Personas that act** (from 2026-10-20 notes): fresh bots score jobs within 180 tiles of their home town ×1.5 (so they spread out round their towns instead of crowding the same cows and snails). Grudges against monsters (goblins, hill giants, chickens, seagulls, muggers, guards, dwarves) score that hunt ×1.4, announce it, and the catchphrase comes out on the first kill. "The Exchange clerks" bots call out hauls from 10 items at 90% of guide rather than the Exchange. "The Al Kharid gate toll" bots mutter about it when they are in Al Kharid (bots already never use the toll gate; which way round they walk comes with the route map). **Casual look:** veterans and the high tier take off their helm and shield while hanging about the Exchange and put them back on before the next trip (counted as worn in the save, and armour shopping ignores those slots meanwhile).

**Giving way** (`AfkTasks.humanOn/humanFighting`): a bot never takes a rock, tree or fishing spot a player is standing at and working, nor a monster a player is fighting (single or multi combat); it picks another and, at most every 10 min, says "ah all yours mate" / "gl on drops".

**Crews** (`Crew`): on spawn, 2 in 3 join an open crew (< 4) from their home town with a greeting exchange; 1 in 3 of the rest open one. Followers take the leader's current job within 3 min ("Wait up, X!"), otherwise wait up to 2.5 min. Leaders count the crew against spot capacity. After a trip the richest member hands the poorest a tenth of the gap ("Here, your share"). Leader's shift end winds the others down within a minute. Crews aren't saved; they re-form after a restart.

**High tier** (10, `-Dsmartbots.high`; remembered high-tier profiles are restored first and all of them come back even if more than 10; new ones are only minted to fill the gap): HIGH gear tier, Att/Str/Def/HP 85–95, Prayer 52–70, gathering 60–90, 120–250k gp nest egg, anti-dragon shield from the shop, Dragon Slayer set complete. Curated dungeon hunts: Blue dragon (Taverley), Black demon, Lesser demon (Karamja), Fire giant (Waterfall), Hill Giant (Edgeville). Verified 2026-10-20 14:00: a high-tier bot (combat 110) took the Blue dragon hunt twice and came back with 77 items. From 18:31 they walk in: teleport to Falador square, walk past the bank to the Taverley ladder and climb down (trial: Port Sarim to the ladder on foot). Inside, the gate still stops them, and the stuck-walk fallback teleports them the last stretch to the dragons until the route map exists. With dungeon distance fixed (bugs-fixed #1), every high-tier bot in the trial chose blue dragons.

**Pointers:** `COUNT`, `HIGH`, `GATHER_SKILLS`, `GOALS`, `capacity()`, `poisonous()`, `magicUser()`, `SPOT_DEATHS`, `SHOP_FOODS`, `MILL`, `Mood`, `Crew`.

## 2. Artisans (`Artisans.java`, `artisan-recipes.json`)
**Who:** 9 bots: 2 smiths, 2 smelters, fletcher, cook, herbalist, crafter, runecrafter. Spawn with a 40–80k float. Work at real spots (Varrock anvils, Falador/Al Kharid/Edgeville furnaces, Catherby/Lumbridge ranges, Taverley herb spot, Varrock crafting, Aubury's).
**Loop:** CHOOSE a recipe (472 recipes) whose product is below target and whose inputs are in stock (planned off-thread from the market snapshot); **buy inputs**: from its own bank first (bought at a callout or handed over in a split), the rest from Exchange stock via the ledger (paying suppliers, keeping 50 of each input for players); WALK to the spot (teleports after 90 s stuck); WORK with the trade's animation for 3 s per batch (max 4 min); output: splits owed are paid first (§9), the rest goes to the Exchange via the ledger; news for batches ≥ 20. IDLE lines when the market has all it needs.
**Targets** (`Artisans.target`): by value: < 50 gp → 150, < 500 → 200, < 5k → 100, < 40k → 40, else 12, plus a demand boost from what players actually buy.
**Also:** they pay the bounty premiums (§6) from their wallets, and buy raw materials at bank callouts (§9).
**Pointers:** `CREW` (trade mix), `KEEP_INPUT = 50`, `target()`, `SHORT` (inputs blocking recipes, feeds adventurers' job weights).

## 3. Travelling traders (`TravellingTraders.java`)
**Who:** 4 rich-looking bots. **Loop (live 2026-10-21 01:09):** GATHER for real for 30–60 min (miner: best ore; fishmonger: lobsters/trout/shrimp, cooked; treasure hunters: chaos druids or dark wizards), banking it; the banked haul is packed as notes and priced. Takings stay in its bank; name, gear and bank are kept over restarts (`ServiceBots.java`). **Old loop:** GATHER a haul (what the market is short of) → SEEK a player → PITCH (hello, goods, price, quip, "once more") → TRADING through the real trade screen (offers its items, accepts when only coins ≥ price are offered) → STASH unsold goods on the Exchange after ~3 min and start again. **Haggling** (hears your chat): a number (k/m ok) ≥ price → deal; ≥ 80% → accepts that price; ≥ 55% → counters at the midpoint; else a quip; two rounds then "final price". Messages to you are plain colour and split into 75-char lines. Since batch 12 a trader waits for its runner to halt before packing (no second controller), a Catherby trader gathers at Catherby (2837,3432) instead of walking to Draynor, and its shop teleport lands on a standable tile.

## 5. Market brain and ledger (`MarketBrain.java`, `Ledger.java`)
- **Stock snapshot every 5 min**; **bounty** per catalogue item (task products + all monster drops: 261 ids). Items something consumes (artisan inputs and products, gathered goods, gear sold in a shop or a goal, cooked food, potions, runes, bones, dragonhides) or a player wants: gap² × log10(10 + value) × 2 if none × 1.5 if a player has a buy offer × (1 + min(1, player demand/50)). Anything else: 0.25 × gap × log10(10 + value) × the demand factor (it still sells, it never pulls a job by itself). **Surplus** = stock ≥ 5× target: gatherers leave it alone, the **flood guard** trims 1%/hour of oversupplied piles (nobody is paid for trimmed units).
- **Hourly:** news (top mover, most wanted), the clerk round, ledger summary; the crier is called.
- **Ledger:** every supply is a lot {supplier, qty, time}; consumption pays suppliers oldest-first at guide; `data/eco/ledger.json`. **Clerk fund:** lots unsold ≥ 24 h at the head of a queue whose supplier has < 5k gp are bought at 60% guide; **starter bailout**: any lot (anywhere in the queue) of a supplier with < 1,000 gp is bought after 6 h, poorest first; both share the ≤ 2,000,000 gp/day cap. Players' Exchange purchases pay suppliers too (deltas from `player_offers`).
- **Price drift** (`PriceDrift.java`, `-Dprices.drift`, on): hourly, the 40 most-wanted items that are under a quarter of target rise by 2%/24 per hour; surplus items fall the same; never outside 70–150% of the first price seen (`data/eco/price-base.json`). Direct `UPDATE price_index`.
- **Demand learning:** units players bought accumulate per item, decaying 1%/hour.

## 6. Bounty board and crier (`Bounty.java`)
`::market` lists up to 8 wanted items that a player can gather or hunt (guide ≤ 25k): guide, premium (+25%), stock, and the artisans' pooled funds; it says what bots pay. The bot buyer takes a listed (bounty) item from a player's Exchange sell offer at up to 100% of `MarketBrain.botRef` (the guide kept within 70–150% of the item's base), ordinary items at up to 90% (live 10-23 10:59; it was 85%, which never bought a sale at guide). When the sale into bot stock completes, the artisans pay the premium from their wallets into the player's bank (per sale ≤ 50,000, ≤ 5% of the pool, ≤ 500,000/day) with a message, a thank-you from the nearest bot, and a news line. While an item is on the board no bot channel sells it (Deals callouts and requests, travelling traders, Exchange bot stock), so a bounty can't be collected in a loop. The Varrock local calls the top four hourly. Batch 17 (live 2026-10-25): the +25% premium is not paid on units the player bought on the Exchange in the last 48 h (`Bounty.exempt`, `player_offers`); Deals and travelling traders are not counted yet.

## 7. Bot chat (`BotChat.java`)
- **Hears** players within range: greetings (waves back, remembers you: visits, first seen, last topic, follow-ups), "where is X", "how much is X", quests ("start X", and **"stuck on X"** answered from your real quest stage and the guide steps), "who are you" (persona intro + home town/grudge), "what are you doing", small talk, "are you a bot".
- **Ambient** every 25–60 s to one human near one bot (bot cooldown 45–105 s): warnings first (Wilderness ditch, aggressive monsters above your level) > follow-up on your last topic > rumour from real events (hauls, batches, market, shooting star, deaths, goals, crews) > a notice about your gear/activity > one of 40 creative lines.
- **Bot-to-bot banter** every 1–3 min between two bots near a human; one time in three they **talk about the human watching** instead ("Isn't that X? Saw them down the mines earlier." / "Yeah, nearly level 41 Mining now."), from what the player has been training (`Watch`) or how long bots have known them (`Memory`).
- **Chime-ins:** after a bot answers a player, another idle bot within 6 tiles adds a line 1 time in 4 (agrees, teases, or warns about prices); a bank callout with a player in sight gets a rival comment 1 time in 4 ("Don't pay X 160 for coal...").
- **gz cascade:** when a bot levels (10+) with a player in sight, 1–2 bots within 8 tiles say gratz/gz/nice lvl, and it sometimes says ty (at most once per 2 min per bot). The server plays its own level-up fireworks.
- **Reactions:** faces a player within 2.5 tiles (1 in 3 ticks, if idle), steps aside when walked into (within 1 tile, idle, 1 in 2).
- **Variety (live 10-21 01:09):** every line any bot picks is remembered for 20 minutes across all bots, and a fresh option is chosen whenever one exists, so the same line rarely shows twice in a short window. Pools were enlarged: 80 creative remarks (was 40), 42 banter pairs (was 12), 27 trip set-outs, 15 trip-done lines, 3–6 options per persona greeting/thanks/goodbye, more idle, mood, death, grave, goal, shop, crew, spectacle, callout and gz lines. A bot that is walking or running when it talks doesn't turn to face you; one standing still turns, then lets go after speaking.
- **Trade talk (live 10-21 01:09, `TradeTalk.java`):** "do you need coal?", "will you buy my lobsters", "what are you buying?" (its real want-list: goal, missing armour, arrows/runes, food, bones; an artisan's inputs), "how much would you pay for 20 sharks?" (guide, and up to how many), "would you pay 1k each for yews?" (yes up to 5% over guide), "do you have any iron ore?" / "sell me your nats" / "got any lobbies?" (its real bank; if it is idle at a bank and doesn't use the item it puts it up for you, see §9; if not, how many the Exchange has), "what are you selling?". ## 9. Trading between bots (`Deals.java`; design: the design notes (not published))
- **Bank arrival** of a 20+ haul (`-Ddeals.min`; **10+ for a bot under 400 gp**, so a broke bot is paid on the spot): (1) **split** if an artisan of a trade with a recipe that takes the item stands within 15 tiles. One-input recipes (raw fish, iron ore, grimy herbs) always qualify; multi-input ones (steel/mithril/adamant bars, potions) qualify when the artisan already holds the other inputs (coal, vials) in its bank, and the batch count is capped by what it holds. The raw moves to the artisan, who owes 85% of the output (keeps the rest as fee); paid from the next batch, or in coins after 30 min; (2) else a **callout** at 92% of guide for 25 s ("Selling 80 coal at 160 each, under guide. Trade me."). After 10 s the nearest idle bot within 15 tiles that will **use** it (adventurer: food it eats and has < 12; builder: logs for the sawmill, cap 27; artisan: an input of its trade) and can pay walks to a free neighbouring tile (**tile scatter**), says "ill take em", both face, coins move buyer → seller, items seller → buyer bank ("as notes" over 20). **90 s** without the buyer arriving → "Stood me up", haul to the Exchange. No taker → Exchange. **Human interception:** trade the seller within the window: the haul is offered noted; accepted when only coins ≥ total are offered.
- **Direct deals at the Exchange** (adventurers on a break): if any bot within 6 tiles has a lot waiting in the ledger of something it wants (goal, armour it is missing, cooked fish), it buys up to 10 (1 for gear) straight from that bot's lot at guide, and both haggle out loud.
- **Player trades** (`PlayerTrades.java`, `-Dplayertrades`): trade an idle smart bot (between trips, at a bank or the Exchange) or an artisan and put items in the window. If every item is on its want-list (its goal, armour it is missing, cooked food up to 56, bones up to 200 while under 43 Prayer; an artisan: its trade's inputs up to 500) and it can afford it, it puts guide price in coins up and accepts, then says "Cheers mate, needed that!". Anything else: "No use for X, mate." Change the offer after its coins are up and it declines. Busy bots say "Catch me at the bank." Bought gear is worn; the rest goes in its bank.
- **Haggling (live 10-21 01:09, `Haggle.java` + `Deals.heard/haggle`):** a player within 12 tiles of a callout (20 if they name the bot) can ask "how much for 50?", say "i'll take 40" (a part lot at the callout price; the rest stays on sale), or offer ("can you do 23 each", "20k for the lot", "10% off", "meet me in the middle"). The seller accepts at 96% of its asking price or better (92% on the second try; mates 92/88%), otherwise counters halfway (counters and accepted bids never below 86% of guide, 85% for mates; live 10-23 01:02, bugs-fixed #141; since 10-23 10:59 never below 92% / 91% of `Deals.ref` = max(guide, `botRef`), #188), and after two tries says final. "deal"/"ok" takes the counter; "nah" drops it. A player haggling holds the offer (no bot buyer, no expiry) for 45–60 s, and the trade window honours the agreed total and quantity. Price words only count when they're about the deal, so "how much is a rune scimitar" still goes to the price answer. Since 10-23 07:16, "the lot" said after a part bid ("I'll take 10") moves the deal to the whole lot.
- **Selling on request:** "do you have any X?" to an idle bot at a bank that has X and doesn't use it: it puts all of it up for that player at 95% of guide (88% mates, never under 91% of `Deals.ref` since 10-23 10:59), held 60 s; unsold, it stays in the bot's bank (no Exchange dump).
- **Buy-window haggling (`PlayerTrades.heard/ask`):** with a bot's guide-price coins in the window, say your price ("120 each", "make it 5k", "10% more"): up to 5% over guide it raises its coins to that; up to 15% it meets you at 5%; more, it refuses; two tries, then final. A price named before the bot has offered is remembered.
- **Errands (arranged meets):** an artisan within 120 tiles that is short of an input asks an idle gatherer out loud (once per 10 min per artisan); the gatherer takes that job and delivers the banked haul to the workshop for 95% of guide (artisan pays what it can afford); 90 s grace if the artisan wanders off, 10-min give-up, then the Exchange.

## 10. Spectacle (`Spectacle.java`, from 22:30)
Only when a real player is within 20 tiles; one show per area at a time; each bot at most every 15 min. On a break, a bot with another idle bot within 8 tiles may start: **arm wrestle** (best of three, Strength + luck per round), **dance-off** (three rounds of dance emotes, scored on Agility + luck, the crowd calls it), **skill boast** ("bet my Mining's higher", settled on the real levels), or **follow the leader** (copy the emotes; slip and you're out). Up to 3 idle bots nearby gather, cheer and heckle; two of them may side-bet. Wagers are a tenth of the poorer wallet (max 2,000) moved loser → winner; rivalries are counted ("that's 3 to 1 now"); results become news. Alone, a bot may challenge the watching player to an arm wrestle (pride only; say "you're on"; your Strength counts; +1 trust). **Races:** a bot starting a gathering trip where another bot already works the same job sometimes challenges it; whoever banks more that trip wins the stake.

## 16. Companions: bots working with a real player (`Companions.java`, staged 10-22)

- **Crews.** "join my crew" / "team up" / "follow me" / "come fight": the bot that heard it joins if it is between trips and its combat level is at least 60% of the player's ("I'd just get flattened next to you" otherwise); "crew/team/party/group" also brings up to two more idle adventurers within 10 tiles ("Count me in."). Each packs up to 12 food from its bank (buying first as for a trip). Joining puts a stashed helm and shield back on, a crewmate's death counts as a death (grave run, then the crew lets it go), and each crewmate has its own catch-up timer (live 10-23 01:02). Every 2 ticks a crewmate follows within 3 tiles (own catch-up timer per bot; paid teleport if 40+ tiles behind or on another floor for 15 s; "Lost you" and home after 2 min), attacks what the player is attacking or whatever is attacking the player (within 12 tiles), eats at 60% health, leaves at 40% with no food ("Out of food, I'm out!"). The kill stays the player's (bugs-fixed #103): a crewmate hits only while its damage on that monster is below the player's (the player stays the most-damage entity: drop and Slayer XP are theirs) and only where the server lets a second attacker in (`isAttackable`), so in single-way combat the crew never tags the player's monster; there they are escorts who fight what attacks them. Never loots: the drops are the player's. Ends on "disband" / "go home" / "stop following" / "thanks lads" (any crewmate within 15 tiles hears it), when the player logs out, or after an hour without a fight. Leaving crewmates teleport to a bank if 60+ tiles from one (paid) and carry on with their day; the think loop is suspended while in a crew. Since batch 11 a crewmate eats on a 3-tick cooldown; since batch 12 a crewmate blocked within 40 tiles counts as fallen behind after 20 s and is caught up with the paid teleport, which is gate-checked (it leaves the crew instead of landing in a gated area).
- **Errands.** "fetch me 200 coal", "get me 50 lobsters", "bring us 30 iron ore": a number is required ("get me a drink" is left to the ordinary chat), the item is read by the trade-talk resolver, singulars tried. Hauls stay in its bank (`Deals.arrive` leaves them alone). At the trip's end the bot holds the haul as a callout only that player can take, at guide price, for 30 minutes, says so, and the player gets a chat-box line naming the bank; it waits there (loiter) meanwhile. This is the one deliberate exception to "nothing from nothing": untradeable quest props that the quest consumes.
